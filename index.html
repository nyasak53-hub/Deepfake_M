ℹ️ About DeepfakeMed

Securing Medical Imaging and Clinical Trials against AI-Synthesized Tampering

🚨 The Problem of Medical Deepfakes

In modern healthcare, digitizing diagnostic data has introduced new vulnerabilities. Advanced Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) make it possible to inject or remove medical conditions (such as tumors, lesions, or fractures) from CT scans, MRIs, and X-rays with realistic precision.

  • Clinical Trials: Bad actors could manipulate outcomes to bypass security controls or falsify drug efficacy.
  • Insurance Portals: Fraudulent claims can be submitted using synthetically modified patient data.
  • Patient Care: Swapped or corrupted diagnostics could lead to fatal misdiagnoses.

🛠️ Our Technology Stack

DeepfakeMed uses multi-layered forensic strategies to verify scan integrity:

  1. Statistical Noise Mapping: Examines high-frequency noise distributions across image pixels to spot blending boundaries left by GANs.
  2. Texture Inconsistency Tracking: Analyzes micro-texture patterns where generative networks struggle to match realistic biological tissue structures.
  3. Audit Trail Logging: Utilizes secure provenance trackers to confirm metadata origins and DICOM image headers.
